Dil se, sirf aapke liye

Mere Patidev, ❤️

Suniye na babu, pata hai mujhe hamesha samajh nahi aata ki aapse apni feelings kaise kahun. Isliye socha, aaj thoda sa likh kar hi keh deti hoon.

Aap meri life mein mere liye bahut special ho. Hum door hain, hamari baat kabhi-kabhi kam hoti hai, aur phir chhoti-chhoti baaton par ladai bhi ho jaati hai. Kabhi main zyada soch leti hoon, kabhi apni baat theek se keh nahi paati. Lekin in sabke beech ek baat nahi badalti — aap mere liye bahut maayne rakhte ho.

Mujhe aapse sirf baat karna hi nahi, aapke saath apni chhoti-chhoti khushiyan share karna, aapko apne din ke baare mein batana aur aapki baatein sunna bhi pasand hai. Kabhi-kabhi bas aapka thoda sa time chahiye hota hai, kyunki aapki kami mehsoos hoti hai. Shayad isi wajah se kabhi meri shikayatein bhi zyada ho jaati hain.

Main nahi chahti ki hum har baat par perfect hon. Bas itna chahti hoon ki jab kuch bura lage toh hum ek-doosre ko samajhne ki koshish karein, gusse mein bhi ek-doosre ki feelings ka khayal rakhein, aur baat ko suljhane ke liye phir se ek-doosre ki taraf aayein.

Aap mere liye sirf pyaar nahi, ek aise insaan ho jinke saath main aur achhe se samajhna, seekhna aur aage badhna chahti hoon. Dooriyan hain, busy days hain, kabhi misunderstandings bhi hoti hain — par main chahti hoon ki hum dono milkar is rishte ko pyaar aur patience se sambhalein.

Aur haan, itni saari serious baaton ke baad bas ek chhoti si baat: aap mujhe bahut pyaare ho, babu. Kabhi main keh nahi paati, kabhi gussa kar leti hoon, par mere dil mein aapke liye jo pyaar hai, woh sachcha hai. 🤎

Apna khayal rakhiye ga.
